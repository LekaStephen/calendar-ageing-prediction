Interpretation safeguards
- Predictions are point estimates; no calibrated prediction intervals are implemented.
- Only the 17 observed temperature/SOC combinations are experimental conditions. Other combinations within marginal numeric ranges are not equivalent to tested conditions.
- Prediction inputs are bounded by marginal data ranges, but marginal bounds do not ensure experimental support for every combination.
- The supplied data support condition-level trajectories, not independent cell-level trajectories.
- This is an academic research demonstrator, not a battery safety or deployment system.
